# Origin

Proof Factory exists after the scorecards, board brief, diligence pack, partner graph, boardroom sparring, and category-thesis layer.

The goal is to turn that executive-intelligence estate into one explicit proof-packaging surface:

- which proof clusters are strong enough to reuse now
- where evidence freshness and ROI confidence are still too weak
- what can safely move into board snippets and diligence packets
- which themes need tighter proof before they enter the next executive memo
